## 1. Overview

FashionUp respects your right to control your personal information.

This Data Deletion Policy explains how users can request deletion of their personal data, what information is removed, what information may be retained, and the timelines involved.

## 2. Requesting Account Deletion

### 2.1 Account Deletion Requests

FashionUp does not currently provide a self-service account deletion feature.

To request account deletion, contact:

**Email:** fashionup.ex@gmail.com

Please include:
- Your registered email address
- Your account name (if applicable)
- A clear request for account deletion

FashionUp may request identity verification before processing the request.

---

## 3. Account Deletion Process

### Day 0 – Deletion Request

When a valid deletion request is received:
- Your account is marked for deletion
- Access to your account may be restricted
- The deletion process begins

### Day 0–30

During this period:
- Account data may remain recoverable
- Deletion requests may be reviewed for fraud prevention, security, or legal compliance purposes

### After 30 Days

FashionUp permanently deletes personal data from active production systems, except where retention is required by law or legitimate business obligations.

Deletion requests are applied to active production systems. Certain operational copies may persist temporarily as part of normal system administration processes.

### 3.1 Confirmation

Upon completion of a deletion request, FashionUp will make reasonable efforts to send a confirmation email to the registered email address associated with the account.

If you do not receive confirmation within 30 days, contact fashionup.ex@gmail.com.

---

## 4. What Data Is Deleted

Subject to legal and operational requirements, FashionUp deletes:
- Account profile information
- Login credentials
- Contact information
- Saved preferences
- Wishlist information
- Shopping cart information
- Marketing communication preferences

## 5. Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained after account deletion where required by law or legitimate business purposes.

Examples include:
- Transaction records
- Order history
- Payment records
- Fraud prevention records
- Security logs
- Legal compliance records

Such records are retained only for the period required by applicable law or business necessity.

### 5.1 Order Information Visibility

After account deletion:
- You will no longer be able to access your order history through FashionUp
- FashionUp retains order records for legal and business purposes as described above
- If you need access to past order information after deletion, contact support at fashionup.ex@gmail.com

## 6. Payment and Order Records

FashionUp retains order and payment records for up to 7 years to comply with:
- Tax obligations
- Financial recordkeeping requirements
- Fraud prevention
- Dispute resolution

Where possible, retained records are restricted to the minimum information necessary.

## 7. Google Drive Permissions and Data

### 7.1 Revoking Google Drive Access

If you previously authorized FashionUp to access Google Drive:

**To revoke access:**
1. Visit [Google Account Security Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions)
2. Find "FashionUp" in the list of connected apps
3. Click "FashionUp" and select "Remove Access"
4. Confirm the revocation

**Effect of Revocation:**
- FashionUp can no longer access your Google Drive
- Previously imported product images remain in FashionUp storage
- Your Google Drive files are never modified or deleted by FashionUp
- Revocation does not automatically delete your FashionUp account

### 7.2 Deleting Imported Images

Product images imported from Google Drive are stored in FashionUp's storage systems.

**To delete imported images:**
- Submit an account deletion request (images are deleted with your account)
- Or contact support to request deletion of specific images

**Your Google Drive files remain unchanged** — FashionUp never modifies or deletes files in your Google Drive.

## 9. Exceptions

Deletion requests may be delayed where required to:
- Investigate fraud
- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect the security and integrity of the platform
- Resolve disputes
- Enforce our Terms of Service

FashionUp is not obligated to delete data where retention is required by law or necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the platform.
